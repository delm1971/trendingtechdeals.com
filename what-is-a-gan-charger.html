What is GaN technology?

GaN stands for gallium nitride

Gallium nitride is a semiconductor material used in high-efficiency power electronics.

Higher switching efficiency

GaN power components can switch efficiently at high frequencies, helping reduce the size of some supporting components.

Smaller chargers are the visible result

The most obvious consumer benefit is the ability to build compact high-wattage chargers.

Why GaN chargers can run cooler and stay compact

Efficiency reduces wasted energy

More efficient power conversion can reduce heat compared with less efficient designs.

Compact form factors

GaN technology helps manufacturers fit more charging capability into smaller enclosures.

Multi-port designs

Many GaN chargers combine several USB-C and USB-A ports in one unit.
