How gallium nitride charging technology works

Gallium nitride is a semiconductor material

GaN is used in power electronics because of its electrical properties and ability to operate efficiently at high switching frequencies.

Power conversion is the core job

A wall charger converts AC power from an outlet into the DC power required by phones, tablets, and laptops.

GaN changes the internal design possibilities

More efficient high-frequency switching can help reduce the size of transformers and other supporting components.

GaN vs traditional silicon chargers

Size

GaN chargers can often deliver comparable power from a smaller enclosure.

Heat and efficiency

Efficient conversion can reduce wasted energy and help manufacturers manage heat in compact designs.

Power density

Higher power density allows chargers to combine strong output and multiple ports in smaller packages.

Does a gallium nitride charger charge faster?

The charging protocol still matters

GaN itself does not force a device to charge faster. Charging speed depends on USB-C Power Delivery, supported voltage profiles, cable capability, and device limits.

Higher output can unlock faster supported charging

A properly matched GaN charger can provide the wattage needed by compatible phones, tablets, and laptops.

The device controls how much power it accepts

A 100W charger does not automatically send 100W to every connected phone.
